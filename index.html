To my Shona, on your birthday

My dearest Sreejani,

Happy birthday, my love. If I could give you one thing today, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes, even for just a little while. Maybe then you'd understand why I look at you the way I do, why your smile means so much to me, and why I feel so lucky to be loved by you.

There are so many things I'm grateful to you for. Thank you for the ways you've changed my life and helped me grow. Whenever I've struggled, you've believed in me. Whenever I've felt uncertain about my future, you've reminded me that I still can, that there are beautiful things worth looking forward to, and that a difficult moment doesn't have to be the end of my story.

You haven't magically fixed everything, and you've never needed to. Having someone who loves me, cares for me, and stands beside me the way you do has helped me through moments I didn't know how to handle. You have made it easier for me to believe in myself, and I will always be grateful for that.

I love the way your eyes light up when you see me. I love the way you compliment me when I can't understand what you see in me. I love the way you reach for my hand, pull me closer, kiss me unexpectedly, and make affection feel like the most natural thing in the world.

I love how you take care of me when I haven't quite figured myself out. I love our silly moments, our childish behavior, our awkwardness, our dirty jokes, our dates, our shared food, and the way you feed me. I love that I can be completely myself with you, without pretending to be someone else. I love how we support each other's dreams even when they take us in different directions.

And I especially love our little world. No matter where we go or what we do, being together somehow makes everything better. You can turn a day that's going terribly into a memory I want to keep forever. You make ordinary things feel special just by being there.

I know life won't always be easy. I can't promise that every day will be perfect or that we'll never have difficult moments. But I promise that I want to keep learning how to love you better. I want to keep discovering new things about you, even when you think I already know everything. I want to keep making memories with you, even when the new and exciting becomes familiar.

I want to keep choosing you, not only when loving you feels easy, but also when life gets complicated. I want to celebrate your dreams, listen to you when things are difficult, make you laugh when I can, and give you the same kind of love and reassurance you've given me.

And then there's our ridiculous little plan: a hundred meets before long distance. We've made it to thirteen, and I can't wait to see how many more memories we can squeeze into our little collection. But I hope you know that even when the distance comes, it won't erase the things we've built. There will still be dates to plan, stories to tell, food to share when we're together again, and a whole future of memories we haven't made yet.

On your birthday, I hope you feel as loved, appreciated, and special as you make me feel. I hope this next year brings you beautiful surprises, progress toward your dreams, reasons to be proud of yourself, and plenty of moments when your eyes light up with that beautiful happiness I love so much.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for letting me be part of your life.

Happy birthday, my Shona. My Baby. My favorite person. My little universe.

I love you very much, and I want to keep loving you, always. ♡
